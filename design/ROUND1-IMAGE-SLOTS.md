# Round 1 image slots

Written by task R1 (round 1, 2026-09-28). Every picture chosen in round 1, the text it sits beside, and why it was chosen. The client's feedback was *read less, see more pictures*; the owner's rule is that every picture matches the text it sits with, matches the site's look, and means something (docs/BUILD-LEDGER.md, Round 1, Owner decisions).

- **Placement is not done here.** R3, R4a–d and R5 wire these keys into their templates; a key may move to a neighbouring section if the curated copy changes.
- **The sentence is quoted verbatim** from the generated content module under `src/content/**`.
- **People.** Adults only, calm and candid, never a posed smile or eye contact with the lens. Beside crisis, trauma, addiction or mental-health copy the person is not identifiable. No child's face (Inner Child Work shows hands only). docs/02 §Subject matter.
- Provenance, licence and grade settings: `design/ASSETS.md` §Round 1 stills. Alternates considered: `design/STOCK-SOURCES.md` §Round 1.
